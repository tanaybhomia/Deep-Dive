# Welcome to the Deep Dive Wiki!

Deep Dive is a Pomodoro timer for the GNOME desktop. Unlike regular timers, it makes it hard for you to pause or skip your work sessions, helping you stay focused.

You can find all the information about how to use the app and understand its features here. Use the sidebar on the right to navigate.
